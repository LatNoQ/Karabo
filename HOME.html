Welcome to Lesotho Tourism

The Department of Tourism works to develop and promote Lesotho’s tourism industry by creating an enabling environment for growth. Visitors are welcomed by the genuine smiles of the Basotho people and the beautiful scenery of peaked mountains, which cover two-thirds of the country. Check out must-see sights and activities: Maletsunyane Falls Semonkong, Katse-Dam, Churches and Multi-day Tours.
