About Us

The Ministry of Tourism, Environment and Culture is committed to promote environmentally and culturally sustainable development, making Lesotho the number one tourism destination in the region through provision of high quality service and creation of enabling environment for the private sector driven and community based tourism development. To become a leading ministry in ensuring a well-managed environment and preserved cultural heritage, making Lesotho a quality eco-tourism destination for the betterment of the livelihood of the Basotho.
